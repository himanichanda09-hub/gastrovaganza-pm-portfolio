# Gastrovaganza — Project Management Case Study

**Role:** Project Manager · **Team:** 5 · **Duration:** Sept–Nov 2024 · **Client:** SKEMA Business School, MSc PPMBD

A cultural food festival for 200+ international students, run as a full project
lifecycle — stakeholder cadrage, scored risk register, MS Project scheduling,
budget and vendor coordination, and post-event KPI reporting.

## Overview

Gastrovaganza showcased five national cuisines — Chinese, French, Indian,
Lebanese and Brazilian — to build cultural awareness among the student body,
supporting SKEMA's diversity and inclusion strategy. As Project Manager, I
owned the project end to end: stakeholder management, risk register,
scheduling, budget, vendor coordination, and reporting to the MSc PPMBD
programme director.

## Risk management

Risk register scored on a 5×5 impact/likelihood matrix (score = impact ×
likelihood), reviewed weekly with the Risk Manager. See [Risk Register](./artifacts/Risk_register.png) 
for the full register.


| Risk | Pre-Mitigation Score | Strategy |
|---|---|---|
| Logistics (venue, equipment, supplies) | 25 (Critical) | Mitigate |
| Food safety | 20 (Critical) | Mitigate |
| Food allergy | 20 (Critical) | Mitigate |
| Financial (no guaranteed funding) | 20 (Critical) | Mitigate |
| Manage resources (vendor coordination) | 20 (Critical) | Mitigate |
| Low participant turnout | 16 (High) | Mitigate |
| Limited team experience | 9 (Medium) | Reduce / Monitor |
| Weather | 6 (Medium) | Accept / Fallback |
| Team management | 6 (Medium) | Reduce / Monitor |
| Cultural misrepresentation | 2 (Low) | Accept |

## Planning & execution

Scheduled in MS Project across five phases — Project Initiation, Research &
Planning, Marketing & Promotion, Logistics Coordination, Event Execution —
with milestone diamonds at each stakeholder decision point. 
See [Gantt Chart](./artifacts/gantt-chart.png) and [MS Project Tracker](./artifacts/ms-project-tracker.png). 
Team tracked progress on a shared board and communicated via Teams, WhatsApp, and email.

## Challenges & solutions

| Challenge | Solution |
|---|---|
| Ensuring accurate representation of each cuisine | Partnered with student associations Kosmopolitan and Diskovery, who had direct cultural event expertise |
| Coordinating multiple external stakeholders | Built a detailed MS Project timeline to track every workstream |
| Managing a fixed budget with no guaranteed funding | Front-loaded budget planning before committing to vendors |
| Driving attendance without a marketing budget | Mixed online/offline campaign: posters, social media, Google Forms sign-up |

## Lessons learned

- Open communication with stakeholders, team and participants keeps
  everyone aligned on goals and expectations.
- Adapting to unforeseen change matters as much as the plan itself — a
  card-machine failure at the venue was handled live without delaying service.
- Engaging external student associations built both network and delivery
  capacity.
- A mixed online/offline promotion strategy meaningfully increased turnout.
